# From AI Chat to a Study Environment

**Study-material-grounded Exercise Generation and Practice Application**

Mohammadhossein Zare · DJSAMS  
Supervisor: Sári Dávid · Project ID: 2026-SD-03

<!-- 2xl -->
## Problem: Why is ordinary AI chat not enough for studying?

- **Useful questions and notes get buried** in long conversations.
- **Exercises are hard to organise and reuse** for later practice.
- **Sources, sessions and study plans get scattered** across chats.
- **Long conversations can lose important context** and instructions.
- **Studying several subjects is difficult to coordinate** in one place.

**AI can explain a topic. A chat history alone cannot organise the whole learning process.**

---

<!-- 2xl -->
## Target user and goal: Learn with AI, practise when ready

**For one student learning from their own course materials.**

- Ask AI to **teach a topic**, step by step.
- Ask follow-up questions, request examples and explore difficult ideas.
- Discuss the same topic as long as needed, without being pushed into a quiz.
- Create exercises **when ready** to check understanding.
- Keep useful study work and continue later.

**AI acts as a tutor. The application keeps the learning work organised.**

---

<!-- 2xl -->
## Planned scope: A Study Workspace for each subject

Each workspace keeps its own:

- **Sources:** course notes, documents and selected topics
- **Sessions:** conversations that can be resumed or forked
- **Learning work:** saved notes and useful explanations
- **Questions:** named sets, answers, attempts and feedback
- **Plans:** study tasks and plans for that subject

Workspaces keep subjects **separate**, with controlled access when information needs to be shared. Their content survives changes of session or AI model.

---

<!-- 2xl -->
## Initial user journey: Learn with an AI tutor

1. Add course material and choose a topic.
2. Ask AI to teach it step by step, with examples.
3. Ask follow-up questions until the topic makes sense.
4. Follow a personal study plan; AI helps adjust what to learn next.
5. Save useful notes, continue later, or practise when ready.

**The student sets the pace. AI helps guide the learning plan.**

---

<!-- 2xl -->
## Initial user journey: Questions stay useful beyond chat

- AI creates a **named question set** inside the conversation.
- Open, answer, edit or discuss questions there, or use a focused practice view.
- View previous attempts, feedback and optional AI solutions from each question.
- Select the **whole set or individual questions** for the Question Bank.
- Saved sets keep their titles and links to the original session.

**Learn and practise in chat. Collect the questions worth keeping in one place.**

---

<!-- 2xl -->
## Continue studying without rebuilding everything

- Find an earlier question, note or conversation by reference.
- Ask AI to explain, update or reuse saved study work.
- Keep subject plans across sessions; AI can review saved attempts to spot weak topics.
- Retrieve relevant information instead of repeating entire old chats.
- Use a **Global Study Coordinator** to organise work across subjects, with permission controls.

**The conversation stays simple; the study information stays available.**
